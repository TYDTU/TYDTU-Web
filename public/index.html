The work

Honest education without panic or shame.

Consent education lands when it respects the intelligence of the room. These programs make space for pressure, culture, digital life, relationships, accountability, and the questions people are often afraid to ask out loud.

  • Warm, direct facilitation
  • Practical language people can actually use
  • Flexible formats for different ages and communities
  • Thoughtful follow-up through the podcast and resources
